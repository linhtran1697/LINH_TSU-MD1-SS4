Facebook is one of the world's most popular social media platforms, launched in 2004 by Mark Zuckerberg and his fellow students. It allows users to create profiles, connect with friends and family, and share content such as photos, videos, and status updates.

The platform is accessible via its official websiteor through a dedicated app on various devices, including smartphones, tablets, and laptops. Beyond networking, Facebook is also a major hub for commerce and news, hosting millions of business pages and groups dedicated to specific interests. Along with Google and YouTube, it remains one of the most-visited sites on the World Wide Web.
